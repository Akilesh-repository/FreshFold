Our Story

Redefining
Garment Care.

We started FreshFold with a simple mission: to take the hassle out of laundry day while delivering uncompromising quality and eco-friendly care to your everyday wardrobe.

Where It Began

From a single washer
to a city-wide service.

Back in 2018, we realized that people were spending countless hours sorting, washing, and folding clothes—time they could be spending with family or pursuing passions. We set out to build a modern, technology-driven laundry service that prioritizes fabric longevity and customer convenience above all else.

Purpose & Future

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide unparalleled garment care using sustainable, technology-driven solutions that give our customers back their most valuable resource: time. We strive to handle every item with precision, ensuring it looks and feels brand new, pickup after pickup.

Our Vision

To become the standard for modern laundry services globally by eliminating the environmental footprint of garment care. We envision a future where every closet is maintained by clean, zero-waste practices that protect both fabrics and the planet.

What Drives Us

Our Responsibility

Protecting Clothes,
Protecting Earth.

The traditional dry cleaning industry relies heavily on PERC, a toxic chemical harmful to both human health and the environment. We've entirely eliminated it from our process. By utilizing biodegradable solvents, energy-efficient washers, and 100% reusable delivery bags, we are committed to making garment care a zero-waste industry.
